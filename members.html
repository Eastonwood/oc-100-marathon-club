創立願景 Our Vision

一百場全馬，是生活方式，不是目的。讓長期、規律、科學的運動成為一種自我提高的生活方式，讓每個人都能和一家人過溫馨的生活，在日復一日的訓練和比賽中進步。

One hundred marathons is a lifestyle, not a goal. To make long-term, regular, scientific training a self-improving way of life, so everyone can live a warm life with their family and progress day by day through training and racing.

1️⃣ 我們的願景與價值觀 Vision & Values

價值觀 Values：一家人、一條心、一輩子、一百馬。家庭、自律、熱愛、科學。善良、誠實、勤奮、堅持。和即團結，愛即力量——不論家庭、球隊、企業、社區，都要連接在一起。

One family, one heart, one lifetime, one hundred marathons. Family, discipline, love, science. Kindness, honesty, diligence, perseverance. Harmony is unity, love is strength — whether family, team, enterprise, or community, all need to be connected together.

社會價值 Social value：幫助青少年建立超長期主義，用健康的訓練和生活方式，走完人生一百場官方認可的全程馬拉松；用真實的跑步故事和前 35 場獎牌牆，給青少年做榜樣。

To help young people build ultra-long-termism, and through healthy training and lifestyle, complete one hundred officially recognized marathons in a lifetime; to set an example for youth with real running stories and the first-35 medal wall.

2️⃣ 誰可以加入 Who Can Join

認同這套價值觀、想一輩子運動的人，不論年齡、速度、背景。特別歡迎青少年及其家庭——俱樂部服務 greater Long Beach–Orange County 社區的青少年跑步愛好者；也歡迎想陪伴孩子、和家人一起跑的朋友，以及志同道合的跑者。

Anyone who shares these values and wants to keep moving for a lifetime, regardless of age, pace, or background. Especially welcome: young people and their families — the club serves youth runners in the greater Long Beach–Orange County community; also friends who want to run with their children and family, and like-minded runners.

成為會員的條件 Membership conditions：

  1. 至少參加過一場全程馬拉松。Have taken part in at least one full marathon.
  2. 目標也是達成 100 場官方認證的全馬比賽。Share the goal of completing 100 officially certified full marathons.
  3. 遵循長期、規律、科學的訓練計劃。Follow a long-term, regular, scientific training plan.
  4. 俱樂部實行終身會員制，會員費下限 $1000、上限 $10000。Lifetime membership; membership fee from $1,000 to $10,000.

3️⃣ 俱樂部為會員做什麼 What the Club Does for Members

其實只有一件：把這面旗幟——以 100 場官方認證的全馬比賽為夢想——深深地插在每一位會員、每一位志同道合者的內心深處。

Really only one thing: plant this flag — the dream of 100 officially certified full marathons — deep in the heart of every member and every like-minded person.

這面旗幟插下去之後，才有後面的一切：

Once the flag is planted, everything else follows:

  • 分享科學訓練經驗與超長期計畫方法——以 16 年、35 場的親身經驗為基礎，讓夢想有路可走。Share scientific training experience and ultra-long-term planning methods — grounded in 16 years and 35 marathons — so the dream has a road to walk.
  • 百馬路線圖與里程碑陪伴：從第一場到第一百場的長期旅程，每一步都有人同行，讓夢想有伴同行。Road-to-100 companionship: from the first race to the hundredth, every step with company, so the dream has companions.
  • 一個像家人的社群：一起訓練、一起比賽、互相鼓勵，一輩子的跑友，讓夢想有人守望。A community like family: training together, racing together, encouraging each other — running friends for a lifetime — so the dream has guardians.
  • 以真實故事激勵下一代：前 35 場獎牌牆、第 35 馬的經歷，都是給青少年的榜樣，讓夢想傳下去。Inspire the next generation with real stories: the first-35 medal wall and the 35th marathon experience, so the dream passes on.

會員權益 Member benefits：會員權益是這面旗幟看得見的樣子：加入會員後，贈予兩件帶有俱樂部徽章和會員姓名的專屬 T 恤；並且每二年郵寄新版俱樂部會員專屬 T 恤一件——穿在身上，夢想也在身上。個人會員入會費超過 $5,000 者，可將前 35 場已跑完的全馬其中一場的冠名贊助權給予其品牌或公司，終身享有，中途可更換名稱或品牌。冠名順序按前 35 場全馬的時間順序排列，先到先得、依次往後（第 1 場 2009 上海起）。

Member benefits are the visible form of this flag: upon joining, two exclusive T-shirts bearing the club badge and the member's name; and one new-edition member-exclusive T-shirt mailed every two years — worn on the body, the dream is on the body too. Individual members paying a membership fee above $5,000 may give their brand or company the title sponsorship right to one of the first 35 completed marathons, enjoyed for life, with the name or brand changeable midway. Naming is assigned in the chronological order of the first 35 marathons, first come first served, proceeding in order (starting with race #1, Shanghai 2009).
