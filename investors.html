Social value and commercial value, walking on two legs.

創立願景：我們的原動力，是讓長期、規律、科學的運動成為一種自我提高的生活方式：以最受歡迎的足球和 24 塊專業場地為核心場景，以主題 IP 與渡假式放鬆為體驗，把恢復這一剛需變成訓練課表的一部分，讓每個人都能在這裡盡情享受運動與足球帶來的樂趣——並把這樣的俱樂部，做成酒店行業不可或缺的專業沉浸式領域。

Founding vision: our original driving force is to make long-term, regular, scientific sport a self-improvement lifestyle: with the most popular sport, soccer, and 24 professional fields as the core scene, with themed IP and resort-style relaxation as the experience, making recovery — sport's rigid need — part of the training plan itself, so everyone can fully enjoy the fun and benefits of sport and soccer here — and building such a club into an indispensable, professional, immersive niche in the hotel industry.

OC 100 Marathon Club 是一家已註冊的非營利組織，也是一個正在成長的運動社群；Irvine Great Park 足球主題運動恢復渡假俱樂部，是我們下一步的商業願景。兩者互相成就：俱樂部帶來社群與信任，渡假俱樂部讓這份價值可以持續下去。
OC 100 Marathon Club is a registered nonprofit and a growing sports community. The Irvine Great Park soccer-themed recovery resort is our next commercial vision. They reinforce each other: the club brings community and trust, and the resort makes that value sustainable.

A soccer-themed recovery resort at Irvine Great Park, planned at 200–300 rooms, with sports recovery therapy, interactive experience zones, dining, and fan viewing areas. Positioning: California's premier soccer-atmosphere recovery resort. One product, inseparable: recovery plus soccer-themed resort entertainment.

六個關鍵詞：產品邏輯與護城河 Six keywords: product logic and moat

這六個詞合起來，就是我們的用戶、產品核心價值和護城河。
Together, these six words are our users, our product's core value, and our moat.

1. 運動 Sport — 生活方式，不是競技。
我們的客戶不僅是職業運動員，也是所有想一輩子運動的人；市場從金字塔尖到每一個家庭的日常生活，核心在後者。投資人看點：客群足夠寬，不只依賴少數精英，需求來自生活方式本身。
Our customers are not only professional athletes, but also everyone who wants to move for a lifetime. The market spans from the top of the pyramid to every family's daily life, and its core is daily life. Investor takeaway: a broad audience, not only dependent on a few elites; demand comes from the lifestyle itself.

2. 恢復 Recovery — 剛需，是課表的一部分。
沒有恢復，長期運動持續不下去。恢復不是附加服務，是產品的地基，也是用戶反覆回來的原因。投資人看點：剛需帶來復購和黏性，不靠一次消費。
Without recovery, lifelong sport cannot last. Recovery is not an add-on service; it is the product's foundation — and the reason users come back again and again. Investor takeaway: a rigid need drives repeat visits and retention, not one-time spending.

3. 足球 Soccer — 最受歡迎的運動，24 塊場地是核心價值。
足球自帶流量；Great Park 24 塊專業場地是別人複製不了的特定環境，球隊賽後步行進店。投資人看點：流量有入口，場地有壁壘，選址即獲客。
Soccer brings its own traffic. Great Park's 24 professional fields are a specific environment no one else can copy, and teams walk in after games. Investor takeaway: traffic has an entry point, the site has a moat, and location itself acquires guests.

4. 主題 Theme — 迪士尼式場景消費，用戶自己就是 IP。
球星到場、賽後復盤、一起看歐冠決賽——賣的是場景和體驗，不只是房間；用戶的參與本身就是內容和傳播。投資人看點：體驗可以定價、可以傳播、可以沉澱為品牌資產。
Disney-style scenario consumption: star visits, post-game debriefs, watching a Champions League final together. What we sell is scenes and experiences, not just rooms; user participation itself is content and distribution. Investor takeaway: experiences can be priced, shared, and built into brand equity.

5. 渡假 Resort — 全身心放鬆，享受才是目的。
比賽、復盤、觀賽、營養餐、睡眠、高科技理療——在這裡得到全身心愉快的放鬆。終極目的不是訓練，是全方位享受運動和足球的樂趣收益。投資人看點：放鬆和享受決定停留時間、客單價和口碑。
Competing, debriefing, watching games, sports nutrition, sleep, high-tech therapy — whole-body-and-mind joyful relaxation here. The ultimate goal is not training; it is fully enjoying the fun and benefits of sport and soccer. Investor takeaway: relaxation and enjoyment drive length of stay, spend per guest, and word of mouth.

6. 俱樂部 Club — 酒店行業的專業沉浸式細分領域。
這是一個市場概念：不是一家店，是一個品類。目標是做成酒店行業不可或缺的一部分，先行者定義標準。投資人看點：天花板不在一棟樓，而在一個行業位置。
A market concept: not one property, but a category. The goal is to become an indispensable part of the hotel industry — the first mover defines the standard. Investor takeaway: the ceiling is not one building, but a position in an industry.

市場與選址 Market & site

Great Park 約 1,300 英畝，24 塊場地，每週上百支青訓隊伍在此比賽，2026 世界盃遺產。園區 Framework Plan 允許園區內建最多兩家酒店。市政府不賣地，標準模式為地租；公開前例 Canopy 為 30 年租約加續約，年租金約 130 萬美元。選址即獲客：球隊賽後步行進店。
Great Park: about 1,300 acres, 24 fields, hundreds of youth teams competing every week, a 2026 World Cup legacy. Its Framework Plan allows up to two hotels in the park. The city does not sell land; the standard model is a ground lease. Public precedent: Canopy, a 30-year lease plus renewals, about $1.3M annual rent. The site itself brings guests: teams walk in after games.

難點，我們不迴避 Honest challenges

拿地具政治性、週期長、需要本地律師關係。我們的做法：先把資金、可研、律師就位，再走拿地路；不承諾做不到的時間表。
Land entitlement is political, takes time, and needs local legal relationships. Our approach: secure funding, feasibility, and counsel first, then pursue land. We promise no timeline we cannot keep.
